## Tone notes
- Lowercase, clipped. A dev muttering proof, not a brand talking.
- Never explain what the terminal already shows — the line adds the *opinion*,
  the terminal supplies the *fact*.
- Punchiest contrast is beat 4: the line says "warm, not cold" while the
  screen literally shows the clone's inherited contract + its own ack.
